Standing for election

The Governor and Lieutenant Governor are elected together on a joint ticket. A ticket needs more than half of the valid votes; otherwise a runoff between the two leading tickets is held three weeks later.

Requirements

AgeAt least 30 years on the day of nomination.
ResidenceTen years of continuous residence in the Irad Special Economic Zone before nomination.
Signatures10,000 signatures of registered voters, verified by the Commission.
NominationBy a registered political party, whose congress nominates its ticket, or as independents.

Procedure

  1. The ticket lodges its nomination papers with the Commission between 1 June and 2 August 2027.
  2. Each candidate signs the residence declaration in the presence of a member of the Commission (procedures of 2015).
  3. The signature sheets are verified against the voter registers. Sheets with incomplete or unverifiable entries are not counted.
  4. The Commission decides on registration by 15 September 2027 and publishes the registered tickets.

Registered tickets

No nominations have been received. Nominations open on 1 June 2027.

Registered political parties

Under the Political Parties Act 2010 a party must have at least 10,000 members, with at least 500 in each of the eight constituencies. Registered party: Democratic Party of Irad.

Candidates for the Territorial Legislature and the city councils: see the Guide to the General Election, to be published with the nomination forms.
