16 November 2023 · TEC 2023/31

Results of the general election of 7 November 2023 certified

The Commission has certified the results. The ticket of Erlan Toktarov and Aidar Maksat received 92.75% of the valid votes and is elected Governor and Lieutenant Governor. Turnout was 71.6% of registered voters.

The new term begins on 27 November 2023. The detailed results by constituency are open for inspection at the secretariat.

D. Borubaev, Chairman
