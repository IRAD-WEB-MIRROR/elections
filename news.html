Press service news

Notices and decisions of the Territorial Election Commission, newest first.

2026

  • 20 October 2026 · TEC 2026/6
    Check your entry in the register on e-Irad

    Voters can now check their entry online at e-irad.org/vote. Until the annual revision begins on 1 March 2027 the service shows the register of the 2023 elections with changes to residence records up to 30 September 2026.

  • 1 October 2026
    Observers at the 2027 election

    International observers are invited by the Government. Observers and candidates' representatives are accredited by the Commission from 1 June 2027.

  • 30 September 2026 · TEC 2026/4
    Preliminary calendar of the general election of 2 November 2027

    The Commission has adopted the preliminary calendar under the Elective Governor Acts. See the calendar.

2025

  • 3 March 2025
    Residence records: changes of address before 2027

    The voter registers follow the residence records. Voters who have moved since 2023 are asked to register the new address with the Passport and Residence Service (form PRS-3) well before the revision of 2027, so that they vote at…

2024

  • 15 March 2024
    Certified Results of General Elections, 2011–2023

    The Commission has published the certified results of the general elections of 2011, 2015, 2019 and 2023 in one volume. See results.

2023

  • 16 November 2023 · TEC 2023/31
    Results of the general election of 7 November 2023 certified

    The Commission has certified the results. The ticket of Erlan Toktarov and Aidar Maksat received 92.75% of the valid votes and is elected Governor and Lieutenant Governor. Turnout was 71.6% of registered voters.

  • 6 November 2023
    Tomorrow is polling day

    Polling stations are open on 7 November from 7:00 to 20:00 Irad Time. Bring your passport. Today is a day of silence: no campaigning is allowed.

  • 15 September 2023 · TEC 2023/19
    Tickets for Governor and Lieutenant Governor registered

    Five nominations were received. After verification of the signatures the Commission registered three tickets. Two were refused because fewer than 10,000 signatures could be verified against the registers.

  • 1 March 2023
    Annual revision of the voter registers begins

    From today voters can inspect the register at their city or district administration with their passport and ask for corrections.

2019

  • 14 November 2019 · TEC 2019/28
    Results of the general election of 5 November 2019 certified

    The ticket of Erlan Toktarov and Aidar Maksat received 90.31% of the valid votes and is elected. Turnout was 73.2%. The new term begins on 25 November 2019.

  • 1 October 2019
    Polling stations published

    The addresses of the 1,021 polling stations for 5 November are posted at the city and district administrations and printed in the Talas Herald and Vecherniy Nurzhan.
