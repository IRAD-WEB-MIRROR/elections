Leadership

The Commission has seven members, appointed by the Governor with the consent of the Territorial Legislature for six years. Members may not belong to a political party while in office.

Daniyar Borubaev

Daniyar Borubaev

Chairman · since 2011

Born in Talas in 1963. On the Commission staff since 1996 and its secretary from 1998 to 2010, he trained with the International Foundation for Electoral Systems. Chairman since 2011; he has run every general election since and signed the preliminary calendar for 2027.
