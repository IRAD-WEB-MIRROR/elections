Calendar of the general election of 2 November 2027

Preliminary calendar adopted by the Commission under the Elective Governor Acts. Elections are held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November; the term of office begins on the fourth Monday of November.

DateStage
2027-03-01Annual revision of the voter registers begins in the city and district administrations.
2027-06-01Nominations open for Governor and Lieutenant Governor, the Territorial Legislature and the city councils of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl.
2027-08-02Last day for gubernatorial tickets to lodge nomination papers, the residence declarations and 10,000 signatures of registered voters.
2027-09-15The Commission decides on the registration of tickets and lists after verifying the signatures.
2027-10-01The official campaign opens. Polling stations and their addresses are published.
2027-11-01Day of silence: no campaigning.
2027-11-02Polling day. Polling stations are open from 7:00 to 20:00 Irad Time.
2027-11-12Last day for the Commission to certify the results.
2027-11-22The new term of the Governor, the Lieutenant Governor, the legislature and the city councils begins (the fourth Monday of November).
2027-11-23Runoff between the two leading gubernatorial tickets, if no ticket has won more than half of the valid votes.

Dates in 2027 may be adjusted by decision of the Commission. Signed for the Commission: D. Borubaev, Chairman.
