Constituencies

Eight multi-member constituencies: the cities of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl, and the districts of Talas, Kara-Buura, Chu, Sary-Bulak and Lower Naryn (with Toktogul District since 2016). Seats are apportioned by official population and allocated within each constituency by the largest-remainder method.
