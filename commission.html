The Territorial Election Commission

The Commission has seven members, appointed by the Governor with the consent of the Territorial Legislature. It keeps the voter registers, registers candidates and certifies the results of general elections.

Chairman

Daniyar Borubaev

Chairman since 2011. On the Commission staff since 1996; secretary of the Commission 1998–2010.

Functions

  • keeping the voter registers
  • verifying signatures and registering candidates
  • forming the polling station commissions
  • certifying and publishing the results

Observers

International observers are invited by the Government. The Government has stated that the Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe will be invited, as in previous elections. Observers and the representatives of candidates are accredited by the Commission from 1 June 2027.

Publications

  • Preliminary Calendar for the General Election of 2 November 2027 (2026); see the calendar
  • Certified Results of General Elections, 2011–2023 (2024); see results
  • Guide to the General Election of 7 November 2023 (2023). Print only; copies at the Commission and the State Archives.
