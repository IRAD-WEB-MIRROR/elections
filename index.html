General election of 2 November 2027

On 2 November 2027 voters in the Irad Special Economic Zone elect the Governor and Lieutenant Governor on a joint ticket, the 60 members of the Territorial Legislature and the city councils of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl. The Territorial Election Commission keeps the voter registers, registers candidates and certifies the results.

Polling stations open from 7:00 to 20:00 Irad Time. Bring your passport.

Who is elected

Governor and Lieutenant Governor: one joint ticket, four-year term.
Territorial Legislature: 60 members from eight constituencies, four-year term.
City councils: Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl.
