Documents and legal acts

The acts under which general elections are held, and the decisions of the Commission now in force.

Decision of the Commission · 20 October 2026 · TEC 2026/6

On the online check of entries in the voter register

With the Passport and Residence Service, opens the check on e-Irad; the service gives name, year of birth and constituency, never the address.

Decision of the Commission · 30 September 2026 · TEC 2026/4

Preliminary calendar of the general election of 2 November 2027

The stages from the revision of the registers on 1 March 2027 to the start of the new term on 22 November 2027. See the calendar.

Decision of the Commission · 30 May 2023 · TEC 2023/12

Rules for observers and candidates' representatives

Accreditation cards, where observers may stand in the polling station, the copy of the count protocol given to each accredited representative, and photography only with the permission of the chair of the polling station commission.

Decision of the Commission · 12 May 2015 · TEC 2015/7

Procedures for the nomination and registration of candidates

Nomination papers, signature sheets and their verification; each candidate signs the declaration of ten years' residence in the presence of a member of the Commission.

Act · 22 June 2010 · № 10-07

Political Parties Act 2010

A party is registered by the Ministry of Justice with at least 10,000 members, of whom at least 500 in each of the eight constituencies. A registered party's congress may nominate a ticket.

Act · 18 December 1992 · № 92-31

Elective Governor Acts

Provide for the election of the Governor and Lieutenant Governor on a joint ticket for four years, on the first Tuesday after the first Monday of November; the term begins on the fourth Monday of November. As amended.
